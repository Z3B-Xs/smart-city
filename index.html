About Amman

Amman, the capital of Jordan, is a city where history meets modern life. Known for its rich cultural heritage, welcoming people, and growing urban landscape, Amman continues to develop while preserving its unique identity. From ancient landmarks to modern neighborhoods, the city reflects a balance between tradition and progress. As Amman grows, new opportunities emerge to improve public services, enhance connectivity, and create a better quality of life for everyone.

What Is a Smart City?

A smart city uses technology, innovation, and data to improve the way people live and interact with their surroundings. By connecting digital solutions with essential services, smart cities aim to make transportation more efficient, public services more accessible, and resources more sustainable. The goal is not just to build a technologically advanced city, but to create a cleaner, safer, and more connected environment that puts people first.

Our Vision

Our vision is to transform Amman into a smarter, more sustainable, and more connected city. We aim to encourage the use of modern technology to improve urban services, support environmental sustainability, and make everyday life easier for residents and visitors. By combining innovation with Amman's cultural identity, we envision a future where technology serves people, communities thrive, and progress benefits everyone.
